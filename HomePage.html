Welcome to the Wildlife Reporting Portal

Track endangered species sightings across Ireland, UK, and Europe

What is Wildlife Reporting?

Our wildlife reporting system enables field researchers, volunteers, and conservation professionals to document endangered species sightings in real-time. Each report includes location data, evidence type, and detailed observations to support conservation decision-making.

Why It Matters

Systematic monitoring of endangered species populations is crucial for understanding distribution, population trends, and habitat requirements. Your reports directly inform conservation strategies and funding priorities.

Evidence Types

We accept multiple evidence types: Photographs, Video footage, Audio recordings, Physical samples (fur, feathers, scat), and Visual sightings. Higher quality evidence strengthens our conservation impact.

About This System

The Endangered Species Tracker is a comprehensive wildlife monitoring platform designed to support conservation efforts across Europe. By systematizing species sighting reports, we create an evidence base for wildlife management decisions, species recovery programs, and habitat protection initiatives.

Each report contributes to our understanding of species distribution, population dynamics, and conservation status. Together, we're building a detailed picture of Europe's most vulnerable wildlife.
