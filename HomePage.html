About This System

The Endangered Species Tracker is a comprehensive wildlife monitoring platform designed to support conservation efforts across Europe. By systematizing species sighting reports, we create an evidence base for wildlife management decisions, species recovery programs, and habitat protection initiatives.

Each report contributes to our understanding of species distribution, population dynamics, and conservation status. Together, we're building a detailed picture of Europe's most vulnerable wildlife.
